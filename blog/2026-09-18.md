# Friday, September 18th 2026

## Recognition you can hold, moderation you can contest

### The week in one sentence

The pieces we said were coming next are now built: the full recognition model with a rationed budget and a quiet thank-you, citizen moderation from conduct record to jury to appeal, the machinery to recover a failed renewal instead of hearing about it a quarter late, and the recognition a contribution earns now shows up where it is read.

### Recognition, named and rationed

The interim gesture — one door, one adjective — becomes the real thing. The panel asks what a contribution brought you and answers in plain language: it was an insight, it moved the discussion forward, it made you reconsider. The three share the same weight and the same cost; none is styled as rare or superior, because the product cannot ask you what something brought you and then tell you that you were not entitled to feel it.

Recognition is rationed on purpose. Everyone gets one qualification back per day, up to seven held in reserve, and the rate is identical for everyone — status, price paid and reputation do not buy a faster recharge. Scarcity is the point: when recognition costs something, spending it means something. A reaction that is free and infinite is noise; a qualification you had to choose is a signal.

Two things sit beside it. A **position** — agree or disagree — is asked only after you have recognised the contribution, never before, so you are not pushed to pick a side before you have evaluated the thing. It stays private to the author and is never published as a number, because "twelve people, seven disagreeing" is a balance of power, not information. And a **private thank-you** costs nothing, touches no budget, appears in no public count, and reaches only the author — the quiet acknowledgement that not every appreciation needs to be a public act.

### Recognition where it is read

A contribution that has earned recognition now says so where people read it: a single quiet line above the post, in plain words, and only once it has passed a threshold. Most posts show nothing, and that is the expected state — the line is there to mark the exceptional, not to decorate everything. No pill, no number, no badge; a sentence, or silence.

### Citizen moderation, end to end

Moderation stopped being a single vote and became a procedure with a memory.

There is now a durable **conduct record** — an append-only ledger where a member's standing and every change to it are written down and cannot be quietly rewritten. Standing is enforced from that record: someone under sanction loses the participatory powers that sanction removes, and gets them back automatically when it expires, without anyone having to remember to flip a switch.

The automated filter was put firmly in its place: it **flags and routes, it never decides**. Judging whether something breaks the principles is work for people. Around that, a jury is now drawn **by lot** from members actually qualified to sit, with a quorum, an escape hatch for a case that gets stuck, and a written record a decision can be examined against. And a member can **file an appeal** — a confirmed decision should only carry weight once it can be looked at again and contested, so contesting it is a real, in-product action rather than a sentence in a policy document.

Under all of it, the plumbing was hardened: the deciding vote resolves exactly once even under a race, an appeal reversal is the single write the append-only ledger will accept, and a contest that arrives twice is refused rather than duplicated.

### Recovering a failed renewal

A renewal that fails should be something the product notices and works to recover, not something we learn about from a chart a quarter later.

Card retries and the customer's own update flow are handled the standard, native way now. A member whose payment is past due sees a clear, honest signal in the product — on the web and in the app — instead of quietly losing access. And the renewal funnel is instrumented end to end, so we can see where a recovery succeeds or drops off instead of guessing.

The renewal and receipt emails got attention too, on the deliverability side, so a message that asks someone to fix a payment actually lands where it will be seen rather than in a spam folder.

### The feed that rations attention, corrected

Discover — the feed that selects from a pool and gives a contribution a fair chance before the numbers decide — had a rough edge: a post could appear right after it was published and then slip out of view moments later. A freshly served post now holds its place for a short grace window instead of being demoted the instant it is first shown, so publishing something no longer feels like watching it vanish.

### Mobile caught up, and the mark changed

Everything above reached the app, and a batch of rough edges went with it. The recognition panel sizes to its content and no longer clips when the stance step appears; the reaction breakdown is a read-only view, not a second place to cast; the thread view scrolls to the composer when the keyboard opens instead of hiding it behind it; a dead control was removed from the profile step, and a brand violet now runs consistently across every call to action up to account creation. A one-time coach mark replaced a pair of explanation pop-ups. A handful of signup and reply blockers were closed so an account can be created and a reply can be sent. Missing French strings across the feed, thread and profile were filled in. And the Republike symbol itself was replaced across the app.

### What's next

- **Recognition on the read side of the feed, not only the thread.** The public line is live where a post is read on its own; the next step is carrying it into the feed without paying an N+1 for it, which means a denormalised feed state.
- **The reputation the recognition graph feeds.** Qualifications now write to a graph; turning that graph into the pillars of a member's reputation is the work that follows.
- **Moderation on the phone.** The end-to-end procedure is built; bringing the whole contest-and-appeal flow to mobile parity is next.
- **Letting the structured post model become the source of truth**, retiring the old markup path once the read side has fully moved across.
